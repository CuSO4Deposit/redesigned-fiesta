The data directory of the Terraria client

- Windows: `~/Documents/My Games/Terraria/`

- Linux: ``~/.local/share/Terraria/

- If "Move to cloud" is enabled, the data is managed under the Steam directory, located at `<steamdir>/userdata/<numbers>/105600/remote/`
