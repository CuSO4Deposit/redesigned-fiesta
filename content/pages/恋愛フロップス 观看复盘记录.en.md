# Spoiler Warning

⚠️ This article contains detailed plot discussion of _Love Flops_ (恋愛フロップス, _LOVE FLOPS_), covering key plot points, the ending, or character development. If you have not finished watching or do not want spoilers, please do not read on.

---

OP

- The home shown in the OP has no new room.

- In the opening silhouettes of the OP, the upper world is Lovelin under the tree, and the lower part is Asahi and Ai running.

- The OP has 4 frames of Dr. Feynman in beautiful backlight, 4 frames of Miss Ubukata, and 4 frames of the necklace and group photo that Asahi gave to Ai.

misc

- In the next-episode-preview symbols at the end, Ilya's symbol is always a crossed ♂♀ symbol.

Episode 1

- Ai appears right at the very beginning.

- The photo by the bed shows a single person.

- Lovely displays the date as 04-12, and so does the weather forecast, but the fortune teller on TV says the date is 04-11.

- When Asahi hears the fortune teller mention his birthday, he happily says it out loud; at this moment Lovely blinks. This corresponds to Dr. Feynman observing.

- The teacher's name written on the classroom sign is **Cho Monfa**; I still haven't figured out why.

- When Asahi exposes his face and is recognized by four people (Amelia, Karin, Ilya, and Ms. Mongfa), he is slapped twice. Ilya's personality means she would not have delivered that slap, and the teacher was far away and should have been relatively calm.

- During the warm-up in PE class, there is a mischievously placed shot of Amelia going no-bra.

- When Amelia goes to the infirmary, she keeps holding her hand near her chest—a nice detail.

- At the 21-minute mark, when the classroom sign appears for the second time, the classroom capacity is still 25, but the number of students changed from 24 to 25. This is because Asahi arrived.

Episode 2

- The "liking" Karin conceived at initialization was sex, so she dragged Asahi to a love hotel. Later she is also the first to say, "Karin isn't worried about anything happening, you know."

Episode 3

- Karin is very sleepy when she comes down, because she has to go out at night to be a magical girl.

- The classroom sign appears for the third, fourth, and fifth times. The number of students is 24 each time. But everyone is present. I still haven't figured out why.

Episode 4

- Before Ilya goes to the hot spring, there is a final shot of a coil of chest-binding bandages falling to the ground.

- There is a shot of a mask being taken off a wall, leaving only a mark on the wall.

Episode 5

- At 21:00 Karin comes back from outside, meaning she went out to be a magical girl again at night. So busy.

Episode 8

- The school in the real world is called **St. Julianus Academy**, while the one in the virtual world is called **St. Kikuanus Academy**. ~~You really know how to name things~~

Episode 10

- The stained glass in the last area depicts the cherry blossom tree that keeps appearing.

Episode 11

- At 10:45, "恋愛FLOPS" has no sound.
