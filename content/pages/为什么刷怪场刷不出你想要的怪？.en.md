Notes taken while watching [[岛主]]'s video BV1EW4y197dh

Enemy spawning controlled by the biome (floor: any artificial block)

- Pure cave

  - Regular cave enemies

- Evil

  - Regular cave enemies + the biome mimic of the corresponding biome

- Hallow

  - Regular cave enemies + Hallowed Mimic + Illuminant Bat, Illuminant Slime

- Snow

  - Snow enemies

- Jungle

  - All bats are replaced with Jungle Bats

Enemy spawning controlled by blocks (biome: any biome)

- Jungle Grass

  - Bee, Jungle Spider, Giant Tortoise, Spiked Slime

- Hive Block

  - Bees only

- Evil blocks

  - Evil enemies

  - - Shadow Candle -> wall-phasing enemies

- Snow Block, Ice Block

  - Ice Bat, Ice Elemental

- Pearlstone

  - - Shadow Candle -> Chaos Elemental

- Mushroom Grass

  - - Underground layer: Truffle Worm

  - Requires that no platform/conveyor belt be placed above (cannot be inherited)

- Unsafe Desert Wall

  - - Underground layer: various Underground Desert enemies

- Spider Wall

  - Top of the spawning platform

  - Upper-left of the player (replaces some enemies with spiders)

  - The Stylist must be placed behind blocks

Biome + block

- Infected biome + Snow/Ice Block

  - - Shadow Candle: Pigron

- Dungeon biome (250 Dungeon Bricks + unsafe wall behind the player) + any wall above the Dungeon Bricks (default Brick Wall enemies)

  - Any enemy killed with a Cursed Dungeon Wall behind it -> Dungeon Spirit
